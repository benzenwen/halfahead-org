# HalfAhead.org Product Outline

Thu Nov  5 00:53:58 2026
Ben Wen

Half Ahead is an experimental community site, exploring the ways that
text and animation can convey more emotion than words alone. We have
(re)entered the era of text. With the arrival of LLMs, society has
become strikingly redirected towards text and chat. Chat interfaces
have been with us since the earliest networked computer systems, but
there is a resurgence because of the immediacy of typing or speaking
text an the relatively lower latency of an AI response to text rather
than images or video. We have returned to the terminal and tmux.

Half Ahead seeks to radically augment the textual experience, 
among humans and AI in a group chat setting. Imagine if Slack had a
Disney Sorcerer's Apprentice's wand. Movie title sequences are an
inspiration as is the 1990's game "You Don't Know Jack".

The initial idea of the community to invite are the many AI-pilled
engineering friends I have. There is a theatrical-ness that I think is
necessary to invite them to try the animation features. This thesis
needs testing, but starting with a Slack knock off (see the
Thinkfishes repo for a first cut). But with a small amount of
animation like in iMessages for some words. The first few users will
be close friends who can give honest, direct feedback but still be
encouraged to use the site. The name is a double play on words. AI +
human. A human is now half a head without his/her AI. Also the world is
moving so fast with AI, that even us elite senior programmers at best
are half ahead of the middle of the pack. 

Some ideas for experiments.

1. How to progressively reveal the animation capabilities and
   possibilities? Start maybe with a standard Slack-knockoff UI, but
   then the UI shimmers / dissolves / liquifies / particalize / flock
   of birds to a mouse move? etc.
2. How to have someone catch up to a longer running conversation tha
   supports speed readinog so minaturized animation maybe to support
   fast skimming?
3. How to use sound to calm, to excite, to intrigue
4. Can we use some of jalfern.com/demos 3d scene renderers to set the
   stage? 
5. The AI toolbox of animations should be (much) greater than a
   human's. What are the more sophisticated animations that the AI can
   configure relatively more easily than a human. 
6. How to engage multitudes of agents and bots on the screen?
