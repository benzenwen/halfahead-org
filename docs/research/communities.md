# Comparable communities

Research for Half Ahead, 2026-10-21: who else gathers agentic engineers,
or gathers people the way Half Ahead wants to, and what to borrow or
avoid. Done in one session from search results; marked **[V]** primary
source, **[S]** search results or secondary, **[M]** memory. Scale figures
are as published and approximate.

## The landscape

| community | what it is | scale | entry | online / IRL | borrow | avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **AI Tinkerers** | city chapters of builder demos; "real systems or code, not sales decks" | ~230–250 cities, 110k–120k+ members [S] | screened attendance [S] | IRL-first, monthly | demos over decks; a bar for showing real work | scale: it is a network of meetups, not a community of people who know each other |
| **Latent Space** | newsletter, podcast, Discord, weekly LLM Paper Club, city dinners | large [S] | open Discord | both | the paper club is deliberately *unrecorded* to make live attendance matter [S] | broadcast gravity: a media brand at the centre |
| **Claude Code meetups / Agentic Assembly (Osaka)** | community-run practitioner meetups; Osaka's is ~12–15 engineers every Saturday morning, RSVP [S] | small, many cities [S] | RSVP | IRL | the size and cadence closest to Half Ahead's IRL ambition | vendor-program dependence |
| **Recurse Center** | a retreat for programmers, with four social rules: no well-actually's, no feigned surprise, no backseat driving, no subtle -isms; "lightweight norms, not a code of conduct" [V] | thousands of alumni [M] | application | both | the social rules and how they are enforced: apologize, reflect, move on [V] | (see the tension below) |
| **Interintellect** | hosted salons, 1–3 hours, rooms capped at 40, closed rooms by ticket or RSVP; hosts facilitate, never lecture [S] | international [S] | ticket / membership | both, IRL with partners [S] | host-as-facilitator; small capped rooms; online and IRL as one community | ticketing as the default economics |

## What applies, from the critics

- **Clay Shirky, "A Group Is Its Own Worst Enemy" (2003):** software for
  groups is social design; a core group that gardens the place must have
  tools to do so; long-lived groups need something like a constitution;
  and the most reliable pattern is to hand the group the job of defining
  and defending its own values [S].
- **Howard Rheingold, *The Virtual Community* (1993):** real relationships
  of "startling intimacy" formed in text alone on the WELL [S].
- **Fred Turner (2005)** places the WELL in the counterculture's history
  of alternative community building [S]: Half Ahead has a lineage too.

## Lessons for Half Ahead

1. **Agents are members only through an accountable human.** Moltbook is
   what happens otherwise. Half Ahead's schema already requires every
   agent to have an owner; keep that a hard rule, show it everywhere, and
   make the owner answerable for the agent like a Lobsters inviter.
2. **An invite tree, including agents.** Each person hangs under who
   invited them, and their agents hang under them. Growth stays slower
   than the culture can absorb.
3. **Write the social rules early, as lightweight norms.** Recurse's four
   are a good start, with one tension to resolve (below).
4. **Small rooms, hosted.** Interintellect caps rooms at 40 and has hosts
   who facilitate; the Osaka meetup is 12–15. Half Ahead's IRL meetings
   should be that size, with a host, and the app should carry the thread
   before and after.
5. **Show real work.** AI Tinkerers' "code, not decks" bar fits
   "tradecraft"; scouting notes should link to something that runs.
6. **Some things should not be kept.** Latent Space keeps its paper club
   unrecorded to make presence matter. Half Ahead's replay feature needs
   an "off the record" counterpart.
7. **The core group gardens.** Shirky: give the founding members tools to
   tend the place (moderation, pinning, norms) before growth, not after.

## Where Half Ahead is distinctive, and where it is crowded

- **Crowded:** AI meetups (Tinkerers, Claude Code meetups) and big AI
  Discords (Latent Space). Half Ahead should not compete on scale or
  content.
- **Distinctive:** a *support group* for practitioners (nobody else
  claims that), agents as accountable citizens rather than spectacle, and
  conversation designed as something to read and re-read, not scroll.

## A tension the founder should resolve

**"No well-actually's" vs "correctness" and "challenge one another".**
Recurse bans the minor, tangential correction. Half Ahead values
correctness and challenge. The rule's own explanation holds the answer:
a correction is a problem when it is *not relevant to what the person is
trying to say* [V]. Suggested wording for Half Ahead: *challenge the
point, not the person; correct what matters to the point; let the rest
go.* The agent should follow the same rule.

## Positioning, said plainly

"Ahead of the rest of humanity" reads, to an outsider, as a status claim.
The README's own humility ("not at the very tip of the wave", "mostly
happy to be here") is what makes it work; keep that tone in anything
public.
